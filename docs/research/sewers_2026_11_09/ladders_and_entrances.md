# Sewer ladders and entrances: research notes (2026-11-09)

Method: web search plus page fetches only. Tags: [SRC] = taken from the page named beside it (read through a summarising fetch tool, so numbers are as that tool reported them); [OWN] = my general knowledge or design inference, NOT from a source. Several important pages could not be opened (see end). Honest summary: shipped-game ladder rules with numbers are thin on the web; the real-world dimension sections are solid.

## 1. Ladder climbing in games, as a mechanic

- Source engine (HL2): `func_useableladder` handles auto mount/unmount and +use to get on; a ladder is defined by a start point and an end point, and `info_ladder_dismount` entities mark where the player steps off. Sideways dismount is possible. [SRC] https://developer.valvesoftware.com/wiki/Func_useableladder (only seen as a search-result snippet; page itself returned 403). No climb speed in units/s was found.
- Old Source ladders: "players pressed against a surface and tilted their heads back until physics carried them up; a slightly wrong angle meant falling"; modern games replaced it with sticky magnetism and smooth repositioning. [SRC] https://games.gg/news/game-devs-learned-love-ladders/ (journalist's summary, not a developer talk).
- Death Stranding: portable ladders at almost any angle; below 45 degrees you walk up freely, steeper locks you in. [SRC] same games.gg page.
- Arkane's design questions (per the same article): do players see hands and feet on rungs (needs good animation), can they draw a weapon mid-climb (needs new animation set), can they slide down, jump off sideways, survive an explosion mid-ladder, can AI use ladders. [SRC] same page.
- Liz England (ex-Ubisoft/Insomniac): "Ladders are horrible"; players kicked off a ladder got stuck in a move state and could not draw a gun. Resistance 3 playtesters walked past a trainyard ladder; an arrow did not help, painting it yellow did. [SRC] same page (second-hand).
- Arc Raiders: holding the stick down past the end of a half-height ladder sends you off the bottom to your death. [SRC] same page.
- Metal Gear Solid 3: the long ladder takes roughly 2 to 3 minutes of real input, with a stamina bar; the skip glitch saves "2 minutes and change". Sources disagree on length. [SRC] https://games.gg/news/game-devs-learned-love-ladders/ ; https://Techraptor.net/gaming/news/metal-gear-solid-3-ladder-scene-glitch-gets-past-long-climb (snippet only).
- Authoring list for a ladder animation set (a mocap vendor's list, not a shipped-game rule): climb-up loop, climb-down loop, bottom mount, top dismount, lean-out idle, emergency dismount (jump off). Ladders are "fixed width, fixed rung spacing, vertical movement only"; viewers watch long, so hand/foot rhythm errors are obvious. Playback-rate scaling works about 0.5x to 2x of one cycle; blend times too long feel sluggish, too short pop. [SRC] https://mocaponline.com/blogs/mocap-news/climbing-animation-games-guide
- Top-exit trouble seen in forums: a climb that never ends unless a trigger volume at the top ends it and plays a finish clip; one-frame pops at the bottom from root-motion offsets; animator transitions with exit time lag delaying mounting by seconds (set exit time and duration to 0). [SRC] https://adventurecreator.org/forum/discussion/comment/14561/ ; https://cryengine.com/docs/static/engines/cryengine-3/categories/1114113/pages/1048880 ; https://community.gamedev.tv/t/ladder-animation-not-triggering-fast-enough/140719 (all snippets via search summary).
- NOT FOUND: any developer talk with numbers for The Last of Us, Uncharted, AC, Tomb Raider, Dying Light, RE4, Zelda, Gears, GTA ladders (rungs/s, m/s, camera, top-mount rules). I searched Naughty Dog; only a GDC 2021 motion matching talk exists, with nothing confirmed about ladders.
- [OWN] Common conventions I know but cannot cite: press toward ladder or interact to attach; top mount is a short scripted clip where the player turns round and steps down while a trigger locks input; bottom/top exit by pushing past the last rung; jump-off backwards; fast slide by holding a button and sliding down the rails with feet off rungs; camera pulls to a side-on or over-the-shoulder angle with relaxed pitch limits.

## 2. Ladder animation with IK

- Split of duties: keyframes give timing and posture, IK handles contact; blend IK weight in and out over a few frames; two-bone IK with a pole vector for elbow. [SRC] https://mocaponline.com/blogs/mocap-news/climbing-animation-games-guide
- Mixamo ladder clip: hand spacing is wider than foot spacing, so hands and feet cannot both land on evenly spaced rungs; fix is IK correction on both. [SRC] https://community.adobe.com/t5/mixamo-discussions/ladder-climb-animation-has-different-spacing-for-hands-vs-feet/td-p/15051426
- Data-driven approach suggested on forums: each rung gives two contact points (left, right); IK on hands and feet runs while the clip plays; adjust pelvis so targets stay reachable. [SRC] https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/c-ladder-example/96004 and https://esenthel.com/forum/showthread.php?tid=11373 (via search summary, not opened).
- Unity: Animation Rigging TwoBoneIKConstraint, vary IK weight per phase (constant full weight glues the limb); parent the foot target under an object offset to the sole. [SRC] https://discussions.unity.com/t/animation-rigging-how-to-foot-placement-on-stairs-or-slope/746376 ; https://gamedev.stackexchange.com/ (answer seen only through search text).
- Root drift: a Polycount poster kept the root under the bottom foot; another saw the animation offset grow the further you climb. Lesson: derive pose from height, never accumulate. [SRC] https://polycount.com/discussion/comment/2408246 (snippet; page 403).
- Real climbers: average shoulder flexion 39 degrees and elbow flexion 24 degrees in unperturbed climbing (arms are only slightly bent, hands near head height); slips happen when only one hand and one foot touch (two points of contact); the moving hand re-establishes contact before the feet move; foot slip risk is highest on vertical ladders. [SRC] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8829586 (snippet via search summary, not opened).
- Climb rate: a 30 m ladder study used 24 rungs per minute (0.4 rungs/s) as the minimum acceptable pace; at 0.3 m rungs that is about 0.12 m/s (my arithmetic). [SRC] https://rgu-repository.worktribe.com/output/246854 (snippet). Real unrushed climbing is faster; no m/s figure found.
- [OWN] Typical rig: diagonal gait (right hand with left foot moves together, then the other pair); hand reaches about 2 rungs up from the feet; hips roughly 0.25 to 0.35 m of body width off the rungs; chest near the rails, head looking up and slightly past the hands; pose as pure function of height: phase = height / (2 x rung spacing), foot on rung floor(h)+k, hand at foot + about 4 to 5 rungs for a person (scale for 2.6 m body). Faults: hands through rungs (offset palm outside rung), straight arms (keep elbow bent about 25 degrees), body hugging or too far, feet floating (feet toe-on-rung, heel low).

## 3. Real manholes and access shafts

- OSHA 1910.23 fixed ladders: rung spacing 10-14 in (25-36 cm) centre to centre; rung clear width at least 16 in (41 cm), manhole entry ladders 9 in (23 cm); rung centreline at least 7 in (18 cm) from anything behind; side rails extend at least 42 in (1.1 m) above the landing, grab bars also 42 in; through-ladder step-across 7-12 in (18-30 cm); clearance 15 in (38 cm) each side of centreline and 30 in (76 cm) in front for ladders without cage. [SRC] https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.23
- OSHA 1910.28(b)(9): fall protection beyond 24 ft (7.3 m); with cages, landing platforms at most every 50 ft (15.2 m); rest platforms at most 150 ft (45.7 m) with fall-arrest systems; old cages phased out by 18 Nov 2036. The older 20 ft (6.1 m) cage rule is historic. [SRC] https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.28 and https://www.egaproducts.com/blog/osha-fixed-ladder-requirements/ (the latter snippet only).
- UK: minimum 600 x 600 mm clear opening; circular covers commonly 450 or 600 mm; covers sit square to the step irons, cover inner face plumb with the outer edge of the step irons; slab 600x750 with eccentric raising piece for rings of 1,050 mm and up; traffic covers 150 mm deep frame; BS EN 124 class D400 for road use; cover sits off the wheel line; 1,050/1,200 mm rings. [SRC] https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Manhole_cover
- Step irons 300 mm centres (NZ detail, UK water authority detail); required where invert deeper than 675 mm; chamber 1,050 mm internal with 600 mm clear opening and 1 in 36 benching slope. [SRC] https://www.kapiticoast.govt.nz/media/jagkl1k5/kcdc_cm_004-manholes-standard-details.pdf ; https://professionals.lincolnshire.gov.uk/downloads/file/1286/sd-5-12-circular-precast-concrete-manhole-type-b-catchpit-pipes-max-450mm-to-water-authority-standard (via search summary).
- US precast 48 in (1.22 m) barrel: cone 36 in (0.91 m) tall with 24 or 30 in (0.61 or 0.76 m) opening, steps at 12 in (0.30 m) vertical spacing forming a continuous ladder; first step no higher than 26 in (0.66 m) from the bottom; cone wall at least 45 degrees; cone usually eccentric (one side straight, so steps run down the vertical side). [SRC] https://lbfoster.com/assets/datasheets/cxt-48in-manhole-specs.pdf ; https://oldcastleinfrastructure.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/15-QPI_48in-Storm-Manhole.pdf ; https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/claremont/latest/claremont_nc/0-0-0-18056 (all via search summary).
- Melbourne Water tables give chamber minimum sizes by pipe size and depth (900 mm shaft for larger pipes). [SRC] https://melbournewater.com.au/media/3396/download (snippet).
- [OWN] Looking down an open manhole: a bright disc at the top, rungs or step irons on one wall, grey wet walls, a narrowing pale shaft, a lit patch of floor with flow channel. From below: a round bright coin of sky, ladder rising along one wall, chain or cover hanging above. Big storm tunnels use rectangular hatches, stairs or a side access shaft that lands to one side of the tunnel; in manholes the shaft is usually centred over the benching to the side of the channel. Not sourced.

## 4. How games stage a manhole or hatch entrance

- Almost nothing citable. Searches for Fallout, Hitman, Dishonored, Watch Dogs found no pages. What I found: continuous levels are hidden in tunnels and corridors; fog over exits cuts needed detail; developers of small games said hidden manhole covers confused players and needed a prompt or to be more obvious. [SRC] https://AllTheTropes.org/wiki/Direct_Continuous_Levels ; https://gamedeveloper.com/design/a-three-level-short-story---the-level-design-of-die-young-prologue ; https://itch.io/post/681364 (search summaries).
- Readability lesson from Resistance 3 (see section 1): a bright, distinctive colour beats realism. [SRC] https://games.gg/news/game-devs-learned-love-ladders/
- [OWN] Suggested staging, uncited: a cover already ajar or missing on certain manholes plus a light shaft; prompt on approach; a collider-capped hole for vehicles and NPCs (a lid that is just a hatch mesh with a trigger and a blocked pedestrian/traffic nav tag); cover pushed up from below.

## 5. Tunnel mouths as entrances

- Trash rack bar spacing: New Jersey limits parallel bars to no more than 6 in (15 cm); Calgary standard 5.5 in (14 cm) clear spacing; escape racks about 8 in (20 cm) with horizontal supports 15-18 in (38-46 cm) apart. [SRC] https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-jersey/N-J-A-C-7-8-6-2 ; https://www.calgary.ca/UEP/Water/Documents/Water-Documents/Development%20approvals%20documents/2019/2018-Storm-Outfall-Grating-Standard-Specifications.pdf (via search summary; PDF itself unreadable) ; https://www.scribd.com/doc/276094609/10009 (provenance unclear).
- Calgary: gratings secured with tamper-proof bolts or locking device; maintenance access required; one horizontal bar per gate. Winnipeg: grating on outfalls up to 1800 mm (5.9 ft) diameter must allow access to the full diameter for maintenance; fencing or handrails at dry outfalls. [SRC] same Calgary link; https://legacy.winnipeg.ca/waterandwaste/pdfs/drainageFlooding/safetyguidelines.pdf (search summary; PDF unreadable).
- Overflow grates secured but removable for emergencies. [SRC] https://ecode360.com/10077067 family (Allentown NJ, via summary).
- Trash racks can pin a person in fast flow; studies still favour them. [SRC] https://ascelibrary.com/doi/10.1061/%28ASCE%290733-9437%281994%29120%3A1%28218%29 (snippet).
- NOT FOUND: headwall and wingwall dimensions, flap gates, hinge details, hasp and chain locks, neglect appearance. [OWN] Concrete or stone headwall with splayed wingwalls and an apron; steel bars welded to a frame bolted into the arch; a small hinged door with hasp, chain and padlock; rust streaks, graffiti, silt line, vegetation; walk-in outfalls about 1.5 to 3+ m diameter.
- Games: no source found for how found gated sewer entrances read from afar. [OWN] Silhouette (round arch against dark), a vertical bar rhythm, a lit door slot or contrasting colour on the door, a prompt on approach.

## Ten rules I would follow for this game

1. Rung pitch 0.30 m times 1.3 for the 2.6 m body is about 0.39 m; keep rung pitch fixed and the ladder data-driven. (Real: 25-36 cm, OSHA; steps at 12 in in US manholes.)
2. Make the pose a pure function of height, not accumulated animation: phase = height / (2 x pitch), so network copies and the IK cannot drift. (Polycount root drift; mocap vendor on rung rhythm.)
3. Diagonal gait with hands and feet snapped to rung positions by two-bone IK; hand targets about 4-5 real rungs above the feet; blend IK weight over a few frames at contacts. (Mixamo spacing mismatch; Mocap Online IK blending; OWN gait.)
4. Keep arms slightly bent (target roughly 25 degrees at the elbow) and hands near head height. (Real climbers' mean elbow flexion 24 degrees, PMC8829586.)
5. Rung centre about 0.18 m x 1.3 off the wall; give the shaft room: at least 0.76 m x 1.3 clear in front. (OSHA 7 in behind, 30 in in front.)
6. Shaft geometry: 1.2 m barrel (48 in), eccentric cone 0.9 m tall narrowing to a 0.6 m opening, ladder on the straight side, first step at about 0.66 m up from the bottom, benching and channel at the bottom. (US precast specs, UK slab notes.) Scale for 2.6 m body where the opening must pass the player.
7. Ladder section rule for deep shafts: 6 to 12 m depth is within OSHA's 7.3 m (24 ft) and 15 m (cage landing) thresholds, so one run is fine up to 7.3 m; add a rest landing in the middle for deeper shafts. (OSHA 1910.28(b)(9).)
8. Top entry is a short authored transition clip: turn round, step down, lock input, snap to the top rung with IK; mounting from the bottom is auto-attach on approach. Provide grab bars or rails reaching 1.1 m above the lip. (Mocap Online mount/dismount list; OSHA 42 in extension; the rest is OWN.)
9. Allow jump-off backwards and a fast slide; do not let the stick push the player off the bottom by accident, and always keep a recovery state so weapons draw again. (Emergency dismount in vendor list; Arc Raiders and Liz England complaints.)
10. Entrances must read from a distance: round arch or pipe mouth silhouette, bar spacing about 0.14-0.15 m x 1.3, door in the middle with chain and padlock, a bright contrast colour on the door and manhole cover. (Calgary 5.5 in, NJ 6 in; Resistance 3 yellow ladder.)
