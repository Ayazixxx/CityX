# Agent report 3: enemies, bosses, AI (aaa93415f5ee49c6f, 2026-11-05)
Lore factions: spirits, monsters, leftover machines, plus gangs and the Organization. S days, M 1-2 weeks, L several weeks.

## Principles
- Tell, commit, opening (house rule: held wind-up with glint + sound, committed strikes, guards as stances); add ONE distinct red tell for unblockable grabs = "dash, don't block" (Sekiro). Never harder by harder to read.
- One question, several answers: each enemy tests one habit (blocking, aim, light, position, teamwork), beatable by 2+ builds; 2-3 enemy types per fight (Doom Eternal, Arkham).
- Packs have jobs and talk: fodder, shooters, a specialist, sometimes a leader whose death breaks them; shouted plans ("Flanking!") read smart and are tells (Halo, F.E.A.R.).
- Counter the toolkit with what enemies show, never by reading buttons: anti-air vs flyers, grabs vs turtling, lingering hazards vs dash spam.
- Dark is their home, light your tool at a price; enemies give themselves away by sound and their own light; some flee light, some seek it (Dying Light, L4D Witch, Metro).
- Scale by mechanics not health: more players = more attack turns, jobs split; every grab has a solo struggle-out and faster friend rescue; tells sent by name and last >= 0.5 s so laggy players see them (L4D, Nightreign).

## Enemies
Streets:
- Commuters (fodder; Ghostwire Rain Walkers): faceless office-worker spirits walking old commutes; umbrella up = guard stance; tell umbrella dips + glint. Answer go round, heavy, fire. civilian walk, guard stance, smoke head. S
- Bloater (area denial; L4D Boomer): swollen body 1.4x bursts into poison gas; tell 1 s swelling + gurgling. Pop at range or light the gas among its friends. synced poison cloud; grenade blast. S
- Riot Line (wall; Arkham shields, Halo Jackals): 3 shields side by side + gunner through gaps; bullets glance. Heavy; over (vault, jetpack, grenade) or round; gasoline under them. shield, deflection, Shoota. M
- Live Wire (anti-block; Arkham stun batons): lineman with a live cable; blocking shocks you, worse wet; red tell + crackle. Dash behind; water jet shorts him. swordsman brain, Self Smite lightning, wetness. S
- Mourner (support; Doom Arch-vile, Arkham medics): pulls the ghost out of a fresh corpse: that body in smoke becomes a new Wraith; tell 2 s rising wail. Kill first, interrupt, burn and cut up corpses. Wraith shader, corpses, dismemberment. M
Rooftops/bridges:
- Nightrunners (chaser; Dying Light Volatiles): night-only roof chasers using the player's own vault/climb/wall stick; howl brings more; eyes catch torchlight. Lit room (they won't enter), torch in face, outclimb. climbing code + chase brain. M-L
- Spotter (sniper; Halo Jackal snipers): drone or roof marksman, red laser through fog held 1.5 s then shot. Break sight, grapple in, hide in gas. Shoota aim/cover, flying NPC. S
- Still Ones (light test; Lethal Company Coil-head): mall mannequins and roof gargoyles move only while no light is on them, snapping between still poses; scraping in the dark. One holds a light, others smash; frozen + heavy shatters. still poses, synced torches/room lights. S-M, no animation needed
- Wireworm (grabber, signature; HL barnacle, L4D Smoker): segmented machine in the cables over footbridges, drops a line and hoists a player; cables sag, sparks drip. Cut the line, shoot the head; friends free faster. dismemberment, rope drawing. M-L; hoist applied on the victim's machine (like Wraith bite)
Sky (hub guards stay the owner's):
- Seraphs (anti-air; MH wyvern dives, Halo Banshees): the Demiurge's angels on the player's wings hunting the fallen; wings fold, glint, committed dive. Sidestep, punish pull-out; guns, bolts. wings, flight model, flying NPC. M
Sewers (roadmap's first enemy family; Bloaters too):
- Hush (stealth; Death Stranding BTs, Lethal Company Eyeless Dogs): blind, hunts by sound (sprints, splashes, shots, proximity voice), lunges at the sound; clicking speeds up. Crouch, quiet, throw a bottle lure, backstab. crouch, wading, voice. M; voice loudness + bottle throw must reach its machine
- Drowner (grabber; L4D pinners, Ghostwire kappa): lies in knee-deep water, drags a player under; bubbles closing in. Dash as it rises; friend pulls you out; freeze the water. wading, ragdoll pull, frost. M
Interiors:
- Squatter Crew (squad; F.E.A.R. soldiers, Bloodborne mobs): gang holding a building: barricades, lit gasoline, grenades to flush, shouted plans; kill the boss and some beg (like the tutorial's enemy). Split them, kill boss, use their gasoline. Shoota, cover, grenade, jerry can. M
- Synth (elite; the Organization, Doom Marauder): android duelist with your full kit; 3 hits of one element raise a visible shell vs it for 10 s; visor flashes before each swing. Switch tools; shoot during the flash to stagger. swordsman brain, Arcane Barrier. S-M
- Moths (swarm; Alan Wake birds reversed): drawn to light (torches, fire, lit windows), blind and nibble. Light off, burn, dive into water. particles + damage area. S

## Bosses (posture bar: parries + heavies fill it, full = kneel for a free crit, Sekiro; today one player's machine runs a boss; raids/world boss need server-run)
- Giant phase two (L4D Tank, Elden Ring): below half rips up a car or lamppost: thrown cars (red tell), long-held swing. Cover, dash on throw. Giant, traffic cars (one becomes a synced prop), 1-2 Blender clips. S-M
- The Hunter (Warframe Stalker, ER invaders, RE2 Mr. X): the Demiurge's hunter with full kit (sword, gun, grapple, dash); comes for boss-killers and god-tier holders; powered lamps die one by one toward you, heavy footsteps. Duel; friends join. swordsman + Shoota brains. M-L
- Floorwalker (queen of the Still Ones; Metro): giant mannequin hunting a dark mall atrium while 2-4 players restore power fuse box by fuse box; lit sections freeze it. Light it, smash it. M
- The Overflow (sewer dungeon; MH, Sekiro phases): creature in a flooding cistern; water level = phase; dry it vents gas (fire = blasts), flooded its arcs shock the wet. 3-4 players. High ground, frozen paths, burn gas from afar. flood timeline, wetness, poison, frost. L
- Switchboard (raid 4-8; Destiny VoG, WoW): leftover AI in a dead power-station tower, floor by floor; hold fuse levers so its Still Ones freeze; break relays at once to drop its shield; carry its "live" mark away from wet players; win = district power returns. L
- Night Parade (world boss; Ghostwire Hyakki Yako, GW2, Warframe Eidolons): night procession of spirits down a boulevard, drums heard blocks away, lamps dying, a Giant-size lantern-bearer; reaching its shrine blacks out the district until dawn. Shoot the lantern to stagger, grapple onto its back (Dragon's Dogma); anyone joins. Giant, Wraith shader, Commuters. L

## Encounter and AI systems
- Night Director (L4D, Alien: Isolation): per-player intensity from damage, nearby kills, chases: build-up, short peak, 30-45 s quiet; spawns out of sight, never in lit rooms; steers hunters near you, their senses do the rest; a group follows its most stressed member. M
- Light and noise (Metro, Hunt): light on you sets how far you're seen; noise (sprints, shots, glass, car alarms) alerts nearby players too = PvP info. torch beams, room lights, voice already synced. M
- Factions at war (STALKER, Minecraft): spirits, monsters, machines, gangs fight each other; stray hits start feuds; lure one into another. enemy pack's single side -> several. M
- Nests and alert levels (DL2 chases, Destiny heroic): nests in unclaimed buildings; disturbing raises block alert 1-4; killing its heart clears the building, claimable once powered; refills daily (owner's rule); placed by the world seed, server stores only "cleared". M
- Public events (Destiny, GW2): repair-robot convoys (owner's idea) to raid/defend, blackouts, meteor crashes, hidden heroic triggers. server-run. M each
- Karma hunters (ER Yura): NPC bounty hunters stalk dark-red players. The Hunter's brain. S-M

## Big bets
1. Power is territory (DL UV safe zones, GW2 meta events): electricity fought over fuse box -> building -> substation -> district; lights streets, keeps night hunters out; owner's rule for claims; blackouts, Night Parade, machines attack it. L
2. Your death walks (Hollow Knight Shade, Dark Souls bloodstains): a dead character's dropped gear rises as a Wraith of that character holding its weapon; friends or next character beat it to get everything back; rivals can too. M. Don't copy Shadow of Mordor's Nemesis ranks: WB US patent until ~2035.
3. One brain, any loadout (F.E.A.R. goals vs actions; Control's Hiss keep hosts' skills): brain decides what (close in, keep range, guard, flee), loadout decides how (as swordsman and Shoota already); every new item becomes an already-synced enemy. L
