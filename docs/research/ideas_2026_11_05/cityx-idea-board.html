Where three separate researchers agreed

  1. Power is territory. All three picked it as their top idea without seeing each other's. Your rule that a claimed building needs power becomes the main loop: fix substations, feed them, light the streets, and the dark keeps night hunters out. The light switches, the power grid hook and the claim rule are already in the game.
  2. Death needs a floor or a way back. One suggests keeping your three best items in an account vault, the other that your lost gear rises as a Wraith of you that friends can beat to get it all back.
  3. Elements that react. Wet plus shock, frozen plus a heavy hit, gas plus fire. The fire grid already proves the multiplayer pattern, so each new spell becomes a setup or a trigger and the trees multiply.
