# Bug sweep, 2026-11-25

A read-through of the last two days' features (heat press and shirt printing; hobos, hoods, cape, cap, coats). Each is to be fixed with a
test; ticked when done.

## Shirt printing

1. [x] **Online, a joined player's printed shirt shows plain to everyone else.** FishNet refuses anything over 20 KB a client sends
   (`TransportManager` default), and a drawn or loaded design is hundreds of KB; the upload is never retried (`DesignNetwork.Upload`).
   Fixed: uploads go in 16 KB pieces (`DesignPart`), one a frame, one design after another.
2. [x] **A shirt in the press is lost** if the game saves while it is in (quit to title or exit during the 7 s pressing). Fixed: the bag
   says it is about to be saved (`Inventory.Saving`) and the press puts the shirt back first, printed.
3. [x] Uploads are sent once with no answer (one within 1 s of the last is dropped by the server), and a viewer stops asking after 10 tries
   for the rest of the session. Fixed: no more dropping by time (the server keeps up to 100 designs a client a session instead), and a
   viewer asks again later and later (3 s up to 30 s), never giving up.
4. [ ] The press screen does not keep the chat, inventory or map out: Enter places the picture and opens the chat; the camera swings
   while drawing after the chat closes; a shirt swapped in the inventory meanwhile can press the wrong one.
5. [x] Clicking a printed shirt in the bag can put on another shirt of the same kind (the first in the bag, not the one clicked).
   Fixed: `Inventory.RemoveFromStorageAt` takes the slot clicked.
6. [ ] Every printed shirt's 2048 texture is kept for the whole session and made on the main thread (a late joiner in a hub hitches).
7. [ ] PRESS on a press another player just started says the shirt is not in your bag instead of "the press is busy".
8. [ ] The server trusts clients too far: no limit on stored designs, no limit on requests, a picture's size never checked.
9. [ ] Rare: drawing before the shirt's old print arrives replaces the old print.

## Hobos and wearables

1. [x] **Online, taking a hobo's three spare bottles gives one; the other two vanish** (the asks share one key in `CorpseLoot._asked`).
   Fixed: asks are counted (`LootAsks`), each answer one ask's.
2. [ ] A provoked hobo never calms, and goes for whoever is nearest within 40 m, through walls, not the one who hit him.
3. [x] A throw cut short by a knockdown leaves a whole bottle in his hand on other screens. Fixed: a cut-short throw is told
   (`BottleThrower.ThrowCutShort`).
4. [ ] A late joiner's loot window lists bottles he already threw.
5. [ ] A late joiner sees a bottle in his hand after it shattered.
6. [ ] The cape flies up while riding in a car (its lift comes from the body's speed through the world), and hangs straight at very
   high speed and low frame rates.

Checked and sound: cape springs at any frame time, cape equip and unequip, hobo death mid-throw, the hood's reset, the cap and coats' slots.
