# What other players see: movement, items, hair, damage

Everything below is owner-authoritative (the player's own machine decides, the server relays) and is tested over real sockets by `NetworkedPlayerStateSyncPlayTests`
(a server and two clients). The standing rule: a new thing a player can do or have is not finished until other players can see it (abilities: `docs/ABILITIES_MULTIPLAYER.md`).

## Synced

| What | How | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Position, rotation | `NetworkTransform`, interpolated over 3 ticks, position and rotation sent UNPACKED (no quantisation steps) | `NetworkedPlayerWiring.TuneTransform` |
| Walk/run/aim/crouch animation | Animator parameters 20 times a second; a copy glides toward them (0.09 s damped follow) instead of snapping | `NetworkedAnimatorSync` |
| Jump, dash, punches, sword swings, block | one reliable event each. A sword attack or guard is a NAME (`SwordStrikes`: `sword1h_slash`, `sword2h_guard`, ...): the owner's `PlayerAnimationDriver.PlayLocalState` announces it, the copy's `PlayRemoteState` hands it to its own `SwordPoseDriver`, so both bodies are posed by the same keyframes; a hit-stop's speed change is replicated the same way (`StateSpeedChanged`). | `NetworkedAnimatorSync` |
| **Gun reloads** (2026-10-14) | a NAME like a swing (`gun_reload`, `gun_reload_empty`): the copy's `GunReloadDriver` plays the same Blender motion upper-body only, the copy's gun lends it its parts (slide/bolt, magazine, trigger) in `MountCosmetic`, so the magazine drops (and lies on the copy's floor) and goes back in | `GunReloadDriver`, `PistolWeapon.HookReload` |
| **Gun shots** (2026-10-14, pistol and AK-47) | one reliable event per shot (`gun_fire`, `PlayerAnimationDriver.AnnounceState`, never played on the shooter's own body): the copy's gun flashes, plays the crack in 3D at the gun, draws a tracer down its barrel, throws brass and cycles its slide or bolt; a shot also ends a reload the copy is still showing. Damage is separate (`NetworkedDamage`). | `PistolWeapon.OnRemoteShot`, `FirearmEffects` |
| **Rifle hold** | a copy holding a rifle holds it as one (`PlayerLocomotion.SetRifle` from `MountCosmetic`): two hands, low ready, shouldered when the synced Aiming parameter is on. Its aim pitch is not synced yet: a copy's rifle aims level. | `PlayerLocomotion.RifleAim.cs` |
| **Climbing** | the Animator `Climbing`/`ClimbDirection`/`ClimbSpeedScale`, plus the wall plane the limbs are kept off and the ledge the hands reach for, so a copy runs the same IK in its own LateUpdate | `NetworkedAnimatorSync.SendMotionIfNeeded` / `ApplyRemoteMotion` |
| **Mantle** (the pull-up over a ledge) | one reliable event (`PlayerClimbing.MantleStarted`); the copy plays `ClimbTopOut` fitted to the mantle's length. The body moves with the transform. | `NetworkedAnimatorSync` |
| **Vault** (also the dive into a broken window from a climb) | the body's path and lean travel with the transform; the hands on the obstacle go with the ledge-hands packet | same |
| **Held items and back items** (sword, crowbar, baseball bat, pistol, AK-47, bottle, spray can, flamethrower, grappling hook, jetpack, parachute) | the owner sends the ids of the items that exist as objects, whenever they change (`Inventory.EquipmentChanged`); a copy builds a cosmetic model of each (`ICosmeticMountable.MountCosmetic`: the right bone and grip, the sword's two-handed hold or the combat sword's one-handed one) and switches every behaviour off. The copy's fingers close round the mounted model exactly as the owner's do (`HandGrip`, driven by what hangs off the hand, not by input). The jetpack's flame follows the thrust parameter. BufferLast: a late joiner is told. | `NetworkedEquipmentSync`, `Inventory`, `Items/ICosmeticMountable` |
| **Hairstyle and colour, skin, paint, name** | once when the body appears (BufferLast). The joining player's body had NO hair component before this, so no hairstyle could show on it: added to `NetworkedPlayer.prefab` by the scene builder. | `NetworkedPlayerIdentity`, `CityMassingSceneBuilder` |
| **Damage between players** | every hit (punch, sword, bullet, fireball, ice shard, sword wave, flame, thrown bottle, poison) is worked out on the attacker's machine against the target's COPY; the copy forwards it (`PlayerVitals.Relay`) to the server, which checks it (sender is not the victim, a sane amount, the attacker's body within 90 m, at most 40 hits a second) and sends it to the real body's owner, whose own vitals take it: block, parry, poise, stun, health and reaction as for any hit. The blow's poise damage travels with it. **The verdict comes back** (2026-10-13): when the owner's body parried, soaked or rolled through the blow, that answer returns to the attacker with the hit's id (`NetworkedDamage.Verdict`), and a parry staggers the remote attacker (`SoulsSwordCombat.ApplyRemoteVerdict`); since 2026-10-17 a held guard's soak does too, for less (the guard rebound, `sword1h_rebound` announced by name like a swing; docs/SWORDSMAN.md). The hit reaction itself is announced by name like a swing (`HitReactions`). | `NetworkedDamage`, `IRemoteDamageRelay` |
| **Health** | the owner shares their health 4 times a second, so a copy shows it (and a copy that has died stops being a target) | `NetworkedDamage` |
| Abilities (1 to 9 and 0: Self Smite on 9, Grow on 0 since 2026-10-15) | see `docs/ABILITIES_MULTIPLAYER.md` | |
| **The stats** (2026-10-14, docs/STATS.md) | a hit relayed to another player carries its kind (physical, bullet, magic) and the attacker's armour penetration, so that player's own armour, magic resistance and block chance answer it; Max Health is shared with the health; a block's deflection is announced by name (`block_deflect`) and the copy throws the same sparks and ting; a swing or reload sped up by Melee Speed or Reload Speed is sent with its speed | `NetworkedDamage`, `BlockDeflectEffect`, `PlayerAnimationDriver` |
| **/pushups** (2026-10-15, docs/EMOTES.md) | the Animator int `PushUpPhase` (0 standing, 1 going down, 2 pushing up, 3 getting up), sent with the other parameters; the copy tips its own body so its palms are on its floor, as the owner's does | `NetworkedAnimatorSync`, `PlayerLocomotion.PushUps.cs` |
| **Super Jump's charge** (2026-10-14) | the crouch while Space charges it is the Animator's `ChargingJump`, sent with the other parameters; the jump is the usual one-shot and the height comes with the transform. Which skills a player has learnt, and their bar, are their own and are not sent (docs/SKILLS.md) | `NetworkedAnimatorSync` |
| **Stepping up** (2026-10-15, docs/CLIMBING.md) | a doorstep or a sill up to 0.65 m is stepped onto smoothly; the rise comes with the transform, and the Animator's `Grounded` stays true through it (sent with the other parameters), so a copy is seen taking a stride, not falling | `PlayerLocomotion.StepUp.cs` |
| **Light switches** (world state, not a body) | FishNet broadcasts: flip -> server (sane room, 12/s) -> every client; a joiner is sent every light that is on. Not RPCs on a player body: those stop at the 100 m view distance. | `RoomLightsNetwork`, `docs/ROOM_LIGHTS.md` |
| **Graffiti** (2026-10-15, world state, not a body) | FishNet broadcasts like the light switches: a finished piece (its wall spot and its paint, 256 x 256 RGBA gzipped, a few KB) goes painter -> server (a sane piece, the painter's body within 12 m of it, one a second) -> every client; a joiner is sent every piece already up. The can in hand is a held item like the others. | `GraffitiNetwork`, `GraffitiWall`, `docs/GRAFFITI.md` |
| **Balcony doors** (2026-10-15, world state, not a body) | FishNet broadcasts like the light switches: a door opened or shut goes player -> server (a door that exists and opens, from the plan every machine has; 8 a second) -> every client, whose chunk is rebuilt with it drawn open; a joiner is sent every open door. | `BalconyDoorsNetwork`, `BalconyDoorState`, docs/WORLD_GACHA.md |
| **Broken glass** (2026-10-16, world state, not a body: windows and the glass in balcony and footbridge doors) | FishNet broadcasts like the doors: a pane this player breaks (shot, struck, walked into) goes player -> server (a building of the plan, a state past whole, at most 4 holes inside the pane, 30/s) -> every client AS IT NOW STANDS (its state and its holes) with where the blow landed and which way it went, so the others hear it and see its pieces fall from there; merged, never overwritten (`GlassDamage.Merge`: a pane only goes further, so a late or repeated message changes nothing); the server keeps its own record and a joiner is sent every broken pane. Before this a window another player broke stood whole on your screen (their shot is only its flash and tracer here) | `GlassDamageNetwork`, `NetworkedGlassPlayTests` |
| **Body size** (Grow and Shrink, 2026-10-15) | the body's scale is part of its NetworkTransform (synced, interpolated, a joiner is told); the copy paces its walk to it (`StrideRate`); the cast is an ability event | `BodySizeAbility`, `docs/SIZE_SPELLS.md` |

A joining player's body now has an `Inventory` with the item prefabs (it had none), so it can hold things at all.

## Not synced yet

- **Clothing** (jackets, jeans, coats, hats): they are equipment, but a garment merges into the body's skinned mesh and is not shown on copies. `EquipmentReplicationGuardTests` exempts `Wearables`.
- **Ragdoll / knockdown / stand-up**: a throw (a combo finisher, Self Smite's burst) on another player's copy is sent to their own body (2026-10-15, `IRemoteDamageRelay.TryRelayKnockdown`), so the victim falls on their own machine, and the copy on the attacker's machine falls too; other players see the body's transform but not the limp pose.
- **Parachute canopy open/closed**, **grapple rope**, **bottle throw projectile**, **vehicles**, **sitting on benches**, **street doors** (the balcony doors are synced, above).
- Death is shown as health 0 only.
- **NPCs** (civilians, the flyer, the Crimson Claw, the Shoota): each exists only on the machine that spawned it. The Shoota only ever targets that machine's own player for that reason (docs/SHOOTA.md).

## Adding a new held item

Implement `ICosmeticMountable` on its component (copy `SwordWeapon`), and add its prefab to `NetworkedPlayerWiring.ItemPrefabPaths`.
`EquipmentReplicationGuardTests` fails otherwise.
